# Why Bangkok Businesses Need Faster Websites

Thailand is overwhelmingly a mobile-first market. If your business website takes more than 3 seconds to load on a 4G connection, you are actively losing customers to your competitors.

At Deft, we don't use bloated website builders. We write clean, modern code using React and Next.js—the exact same technology stack used by companies like Netflix and TikTok—to ensure your website loads instantly.

## The Cost of a Slow Website

1. **Lost Conversions**: A 1-second delay in mobile load times can impact conversion rates by up to 20%.
2. **Poor SEO**: Google actively penalizes slow websites. If your site is slow, it won't appear on the first page when locals search for your services.
3. **Brand Perception**: A slow, glitchy website immediately signals a lack of professionalism.
